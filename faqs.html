INQUIRIES & DETAILS

FREQUENTLY ASKED

What makes your filter coffee extraction different? +
We use estate-grade Arabica and Robusta beans roasted in micro-lots, brewed through traditional stainless-steel gravity filters for a full-bodied, low-acidity decoction.
Do you have dairy-free milk alternatives? +
Yes! We offer barista-grade oat milk and almond milk for all hot brews, nitro colds, and lattes.
Can I purchase freshly roasted beans or ground powder? +
Yes, our freshly roasted whole beans and customizable grind powders (French Press, AeroPress, South Indian Filter) are packed on order at the cafe counter.
